Iwamura Brewery

岩村醸造

A small historic-town brewery whose internal trolley track and accessible tasting make Iwamura’s sake production unusually tangible.

Why it is worth considering

It is a compact, under-recognized sake stop where industrial layout and town history are still visible.

About

Iwamura Brewery occupies a long narrow historic property where a rail track once moved rice and sake through the brewery. The site complements the preserved castle town and offers direct access to the Onna Joshu brand.

Location: 岩村醸造 — Ena, Gifu · Ena · Gifu · Japan
