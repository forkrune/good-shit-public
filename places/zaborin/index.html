About

Detached architecture, primeval forest and deep snow combine with 100% self-source kakenagashi water. The qualifying private-bath arrangement is: All 15 independent rooms; each has a spacious indoor bath plus a hinoki or hollowed-stone outdoor bath. Water treatment is recorded as undiluted kakenagashi. Couples privacy is assessed as exceptional seclusion, with scenery/openness rated exceptional. Very expensive, especially during ski and holiday peaks; room tub and view angle vary.

Location: Kutchan / Hanazono · Hokkaido · Japan
