Funamachi

ふなまち

The ultra-soft, egg-rich texture and dashi dipping make authentic akashiyaki structurally different from takoyaki despite superficial similarity.

Akashi · Hyogo · Japan

Why it clears the bar

Why go

Its strongest decision-relevant feature is access to Akashi's soft egg-rich dumpling eaten with clear dashi. The recommended visit is: Order one tray, taste plain first, then dip briefly in dashi without letting every piece collapse.
