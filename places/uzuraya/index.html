Uzuraya

うずら家

The shop offers carefully handled soba and mountain accompaniments in the shrine village where the local serving style and buckwheat identity make cultural sense.

Editorial verdict

Why go

Its strongest decision-relevant feature is access to Togakushi soba in its mountain pilgrimage landscape. The recommended visit is: Arrive early, order plain cold soba plus one mountain-vegetable or mushroom side.
