Yamato Koji Park and Hakko Shokubijin Restaurant

ヤマト・糀パーク／発酵食美人食堂

A visitor-friendly working fermenter in Kanazawa’s Ōno brewing district where kōji, miso and soy sauce can be learned, tasted and eaten in a coherent lunch.

Why it is worth considering

It makes a complex fermentation ecosystem legible without turning the producer into a superficial factory attraction.

About

Yamato operates a cluster of shop, tasting, guided storehouse visit and fermentation-focused restaurant spaces around its historic premises. The strongest visit links a short brewery explanation with a set meal that uses kōji seasonings structurally rather than as a token health theme.

Why it matters

Food / cultural background
Kanazawa’s coastal Ōno district has produced soy sauce and related ferments for centuries; Yamato was founded in 1911 and now interprets the craft unusually openly.
