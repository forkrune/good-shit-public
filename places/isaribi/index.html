About

One of the best price-to-view ratios among genuine source-fed ocean rooms. The qualifying private-bath arrangement is: All twelve rooms have an ocean-facing self-source kakenagashi outdoor bath. Water treatment is recorded as undiluted kakenagashi. Couples privacy is assessed as mostly private, with scenery/openness rated exceptional. Some tubs are compact for two and terrace adjacency creates unresolved side/acoustic risk.

Location: Higashi-Izu / Okawa · Shizuoka · Japan
