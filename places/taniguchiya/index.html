Taniguchiya

谷口屋

A destination built around one exceptional object: an enormous, carefully fried slab of tofu with a crisp exterior and moist layered center.

Why it is worth considering

The item is distinctive enough to justify the rural drive and demonstrates how a humble tofu product can become specialist cuisine.

About

Taniguchiya specializes in Takeda-style aburaage, much thicker than the common supermarket product. The frying technique creates dramatic texture while keeping the soybean flavor present.

Location: 谷口屋 — Sakai, Fukui · Sakai · Fukui · Japan
