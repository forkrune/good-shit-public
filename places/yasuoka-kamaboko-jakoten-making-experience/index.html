Yasuoka Kamaboko Jakoten-Making Experience

安岡蒲鉾 じゃこ天作り体験

A concise producer workshop where whole small fish are transformed into Uwajima’s dark, textured jakoten rather than an anonymous smooth fish cake.

Why it is worth considering

It turns an inexpensive station snack into a clear lesson in local fisheries, whole-fish use and texture.

About

Yasuoka guides participants through shaping and frying fish paste characteristic of southern Ehime. The finished product retains more skin, bone and mineral character than refined white kamaboko, making the regional difference immediately edible.

Location: 安岡蒲鉾 じゃこ天作り体験 — Uwajima, Ehime · Uwajima · Ehime · Japan
