About

The menu makes it possible to compare island sushi, pungent fermented fish, ashitaba and local shochu rather than reducing Hachijo to one signature dish. The menu is evaluated as a social eating system with alcohol context, not as a list of isolated dishes. The mapped entity is specifically Hachijojima local-cuisine izakaya; other branches are not counted separately.
