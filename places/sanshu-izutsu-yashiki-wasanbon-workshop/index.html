Sanshu Izutsu Yashiki Wasanbon Workshop

讃州井筒屋敷 和三盆型抜き体験

A low-barrier chance to press fine-grained local wasanbon sugar into traditional wooden moulds inside a preserved soy-sauce and sake merchant complex.

Why it is worth considering

It combines edible craft, regional sugar history and a meaningful building without requiring a formal wagashi-school commitment.

About

Izutsu Yashiki is a restored Hiketa merchant residence with craft spaces, displays and workshops. The wasanbon activity is the strongest food component because visitors handle the unusually fine sugar and learn why its texture works for dry moulded sweets.

Location: 讃州井筒屋敷 和三盆型抜き体験 — Higashikagawa, Kagawa · Higashikagawa · Kagawa · Japan

Confirm the workshop timetable, choose traditional seasonal moulds, and compare the finished sugar texture with ordinary granulated sugar.

Why it matters

Food / cultural background
Wasanbon is made in eastern Kagawa and Tokushima from local sugarcane through repeated kneading and refining, producing very fine crystals and a delicate molasses aroma.
