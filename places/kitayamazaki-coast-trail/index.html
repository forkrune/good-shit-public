Kitayamazaki Coast Trail

北山崎海岸トレイル

The worthwhile version of famous Kitayamazaki is a strenuous trail through forest, fishing settlements, stairs and 200 m-class sea cliffs rather than a drive-up lookout.

Why it is worth considering

Walking converts a famous static panorama into one of the strongest under-recognized mainland sea-cliff journeys found in the research. The sustained physical relationship with the coast is the reason to allocate a full day or two.

About

Northern Sanriku's coast repeatedly hides and reveals the sea as the Michinoku Coastal Trail climbs and descends through cliff-top forest, coves and settlements. Around Tsukuehama, historic fishing access adds hand-cut passages and local landscape history. Official stages around Tanohata–Kitayamazaki and north toward Fudai are physically harder than their kilometre counts suggest because of accumulated ascent and stairs.

Location: Kitayamazaki and northern Tanohata/Fudai coast · Tanohata · Iwate · Japan
