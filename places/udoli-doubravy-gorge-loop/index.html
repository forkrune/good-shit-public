Údolí Doubravy gorge loop

Údolí Doubravy

A proper river-gorge hike with boulders, rapids, cascades and rough riverside trail, paired with a higher return route instead of a simple waterfall out-and-back.

Why it is worth considering

The gorge offers substantially more hiking substance than many Czech waterfall outings and earns its longer Prague journey. The walking itself remains the attraction rather than merely transportation to one photogenic cascade.

About

The loop runs from Chotěboř through the Doubrava gorge toward Bílek and returns on higher terrain. Several kilometres closely follow the river through boulder-filled and eroded gorge scenery with occasional chains or protected passages. Normal post-rain flow improves the atmosphere, but heavy rain can put sections of the riverside path under water and winter ice materially increases difficulty.

Location: Chotěboř · Kraj Vysočina · Czechia
