Kyodogakusha Shintoku Farm Mintal

Why it is worth considering

This is destination-worthy even for travellers who normally ignore farm shops: the products are distinctive, technically serious and difficult to experience as completely elsewhere.

About

Kyodogakusha Shintoku Farm is a working cooperative farm and cheese producer, not a themed attraction. Mintal provides direct tasting and retail access to cheeses whose microbial character and maturation are inseparable from the Tokachi environment.

Recommended order / experience
Taste several cheeses at Mintal, prioritize the seasonally available washed rind and raclette styles, and leave time to understand the farm’s production philosophy.

Why it matters

Food / cultural background
The farm’s cooperative model and natural maturation helped establish Hokkaidō artisan cheese internationally; Sakura became a landmark Japanese cheese.
