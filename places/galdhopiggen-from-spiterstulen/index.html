Galdhøpiggen from Spiterstulen

Galdhøpiggen fra Spiterstulen

Norway's highest summit by the long Spiterstulen approach, avoiding the glacier crossing used by the shorter Juvasshytta route.

Bøverdalen / Jotunheimen · Innlandet · Norway

Why go

  • This is a very demanding high-mountain day with roughly 1,450 m ascent, extensive boulder terrain and rapid weather changes; turn back early if conditions deteriorate.
  • Snow and ice can persist in summer. The separate route from Juvasshytta crosses Styggebreen and requires glacier guidance; it is outside this variant.
  • The Good Shit line is descriptive and non-navigation-grade; current signs, weather, closures and advice from Spiterstulen take precedence.
