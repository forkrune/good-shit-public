Chiburijima Island Circuit

知夫里島

A compact whole-island journey links nearly treeless pasture, cattle and horses, old stone walls, villages and oxidized red volcanic sea cliffs.

Why it is worth considering

Unlike an island built around a single viewpoint, Chiburijima combines pasture history, volcanic geology and settlement in one continuous small-scale journey. It is a strong reason not to stop Oki research at Nishinoshima.

About

Chiburijima, the smallest inhabited Dōzen island, works because its landscapes remain coherent while moving through them. Akahage-yama opens into broad grazed uplands, old stone walls and ocean views before roads descend toward the vividly oxidized Akakabe cliff and small settlements. A roughly 27 km circuit is practical by reserved scooter or car; cycling is possible but exposed and hilly.
