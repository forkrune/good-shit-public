Why it is worth considering

It is a polished but still food-led window into Hokkaidō confectionery ingredients and product development.

About

Rokkatei’s Obihiro flagship combines retail with café service in the brand’s regional heartland. Familiar products are available nationwide, but fresh desserts and the Tokachi context make the main store a stronger experience.
