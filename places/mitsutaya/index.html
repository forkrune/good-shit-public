Mitsutaya

満田屋

An unusually coherent Aizu meal built around skewered tofu, mochi, herring and vegetables grilled over charcoal and brushed with miso.

Why it is worth considering

It compresses several Aizu food traditions into one focused, affordable experience with a strong sense of place.

About

Mitsutaya occupies a historic miso merchant property and specializes in charcoal-grilled dengaku. The hearth, skewers and Aizu miso are functional parts of the meal rather than decorative staging.

Location: 満田屋 — Aizuwakamatsu, Fukushima · Aizuwakamatsu · Fukushima · Japan

Why it matters

Food / cultural background
Dengaku evolved from skewered foods grilled with miso; Aizu’s fermentation culture gives the style a particularly natural home.
