Kunisaki Minemichi Long Trail

国東半島峯道ロングトレイル

Old ascetic paths link cave temples, cliff Buddhas, volcanic rock, farm basins and village roads into a sacred cultural landscape that works best as selected sections.

Why it is worth considering

Few routes integrate geology, active religious heritage and lived rural landscape as densely. T1 is the clearest introduction; more rugged sections around T4/K1 add stronger ascetic atmosphere.

About

The modern trail network extends roughly 135 km across ten sections, following terrain associated with the Rokugō Manzan ascetic tradition. Strong sections repeatedly combine rock sanctuaries, carved Buddhas, temples, ridges and old agricultural landscapes. The research does not recommend completionism: road walking, sparse resupply and uneven section quality make one to three carefully chosen stages a better visitor experience.

Location: Kunisaki Peninsula, centered on Bungotakada/Kunisaki · Kunisaki Peninsula · Oita · Japan
