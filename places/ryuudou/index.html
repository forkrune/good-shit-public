About

No other affordable finalist offers comparable freedom to sample many source-fed outdoor baths throughout the stay. The qualifying private-bath arrangement is: All staying guests receive free, 24-hour, time-unlimited use of 18 private outdoor baths whenever each bath is vacant; some rooms add private baths. Water treatment is recorded as undiluted kakenagashi. Couples privacy is assessed as highly private, with scenery/openness rated strong. Facilities are less polished than luxury finalists and a particular bath is not guaranteed at a particular moment.

Location: Yunokoya Onsen / Minakami · Gunma · Japan
