About

A modest traditional pub with a genuinely destination-worthy signature dish. The quarter duck is stuffed with potato pancakes, so the rendered fat seasons the filling; sauerkraut supplies the needed acidity. It remained exceptionally inexpensive on the verification date. Operational status: Confirmed open; verified 2026-08-07. Official site live; same-day dated menu; current Firmy/Mapy reviews corroborate operation. Potential drawbacks: Cash-only reports persist; traditional room/service; full-menu timing is easy to misunderstand.
