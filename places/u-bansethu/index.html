U Bansethů

A modest traditional pub with a genuinely destination-worthy signature dish.

Prague · Czechia

Show on map Report / recheck
Why it clears the bar

Why go

The quarter duck is stuffed with potato pancakes, so the rendered fat seasons the filling; sauerkraut supplies the needed acidity. It remained exceptionally inexpensive on the verification date. Order: Čtvrtka dozlatova pečené kachničky plněné bramborovými plackami a kysaným zelím; optionally beef broth or tripe soup. Why: The duck has a longstanding dish-specific reputation and was still listed for 209 CZK on the dated 2026-08-07 menu.
