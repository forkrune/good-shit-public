Miune–Tenguzuka–Ushi-no-Se Ridge

三嶺–天狗塚–牛の背

Long open sasa ridges, isolated ponds and broad undeveloped horizons make the quieter western Tsurugi range a superb clear-weather day for a strong hiker.

Why it is worth considering

Experienced Japanese hikers repeatedly singled out the western extension for solitude and sustained ridge quality rather than one summit. On a stable clear day it is a destination-worthy mountain journey.

About

After the forest climb toward Miune, the western ridge becomes unusually open: rounded bamboo-grass slopes continue through Tenguzuka to the broad Ushi-no-Se upland with little visible development. Practical versions run roughly 16–21 km and commonly require a shuttle or two vehicles. The route's value is highly weather-dependent because fog removes the continuous-ridge panorama that makes it exceptional.

Location: Western Tsurugi range around Miune and Tenguzuka · Miyoshi / Kami · Tokushima / Kochi · Japan
