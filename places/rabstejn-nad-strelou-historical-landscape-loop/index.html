Rabštejn nad Střelou historical-landscape loop

Naučná stezka Rabštejn

A dense small-town and river-valley loop combining Rabštejn’s historic fabric with ruins, Střela scenery, old slate workings, mills and fortification traces.

Why it is worth considering

A very good layered cultural-landscape walk and useful catalogue entry, but deliberately retained at B/D1: the actual drive is longer than the experience’s general-purpose draw, unless the visitor is particularly interested in historical landscape and settlement history.

About

The marked Rabštejn nature trail links the tiny historic settlement with the Střela valley, castle and fortification remnants, old slate quarrying, mills and viewpoints. Its strength is density and layering rather than one spectacular natural feature. From Prague it is a good historical-landscape outing, but not as strong a pure travel-return anomaly as Drbákov, Ralsko or Bořeň.
