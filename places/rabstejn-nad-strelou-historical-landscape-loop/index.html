Rabštejn nad Střelou historical-landscape loop

Naučná stezka Rabštejn

A dense historic-town and Střela-valley circuit combining a medieval bridge, ruins, mills, quarrying traces and a remarkably intact small settlement with a satisfying riverside walk.

Why it is worth considering

Upgraded to A/D2 after the later research pass established a stronger complete circuit and the density of surviving historical landscape. It is less monumental than Loket or Křivoklát but unusually coherent as a walk, with substantially lower visitor pressure.

About

The recommended loop starts in Rabštejn nad Střelou, leaves the tiny historic core for the Střela valley, then returns through the landscape of old mills, paths, quarrying and fortification traces. The appeal comes from how tightly the built and natural layers interlock: the medieval bridge, ruins and town are not separate stops but part of the same valley journey. Private mill properties should be respected and no abandoned structure needs to be entered.
