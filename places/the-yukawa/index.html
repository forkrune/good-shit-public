About

One of Japan’s strongest modern river-retreat designs, especially in the detached villa category. The qualifying private-bath arrangement is: All eleven suites have river-facing outdoor baths; the three fully detached Villa Suites provide the strongest privacy. Water treatment is recorded as undiluted kakenagashi. Couples privacy is assessed as highly private, with scenery/openness rated exceptional. Very expensive; the best privacy requires a Villa Suite, and pair pricing must not be confused with per-person listings.
