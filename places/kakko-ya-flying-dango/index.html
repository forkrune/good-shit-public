About

The simple dango is inseparable from an ingenious gorge-crossing delivery ritual that has become a living local food custom. Portability, timing and place of consumption are part of the foodway, not incidental logistics. The mapped entity is specifically Genbikei gorge counter and cable-basket delivery point; other branches are not counted separately.

Location: Genbikei gorge counter and cable-basket delivery point · Ichinoseki · Iwate · Japan
