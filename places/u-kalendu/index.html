Why it is worth considering

It treats overlooked cuts as primary food rather than curiosities and combines them with unusually serious fermentation, bread and pastry work. Order: Duck gizzards or the current offal/pig-part preparation; one house bread/pastry item; a slow-cooked Czech main. Why: Those choices expose stock work, fat management, long cooking and baking—the differentiating skills.
