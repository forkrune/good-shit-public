ヨーロッパ軒 総本店

The canonical reference for Fukui’s sauce katsudon: thin pork cutlets dipped in a tangy Worcestershire-style sauce.

Why it is worth considering

It is affordable, specific and culturally useful, though not worth an extreme detour for diners indifferent to fried pork.

About

Europe-ken’s main store serves the local katsudon without egg, using thin cutlets and a sharp-sweet sauce. The dish is simple and sits squarely in Fukui’s yōshoku history.
