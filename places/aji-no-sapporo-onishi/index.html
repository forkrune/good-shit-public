Why it is worth considering

This is a specialist cultural stop rather than one of Japan’s greatest ramen bowls; it earns inclusion because the style is genuinely local and memorable.

About

Aji no Sapporo Onishi specializes in a local ramen style combining miso, curry, milk and butter. The dish sounds novelty-driven, but the dairy rounds the curry and miso into a balanced, warming bowl.

Location: 味の札幌 大西 — Aomori, Aomori · Aomori · Aomori · Japan
