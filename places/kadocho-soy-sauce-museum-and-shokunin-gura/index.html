About

The small buildings make soy-sauce brewing legible inside the townscape that claims a foundational role in Japanese shoyu history. The key value lies in observing transformation over time—microbial, enzymatic, oxidative or preservative—and then tasting the result in context. The mapped entity is specifically Yuasa historic brewery/museum buildings; other branches are not counted separately.
