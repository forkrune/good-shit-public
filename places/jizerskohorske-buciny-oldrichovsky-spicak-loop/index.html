Jizerskohorské bučiny: Oldřichovský Špičák loop

Jizerskohorské bučiny – Oldřichovský Špičák a Skalní hrad

A steep old-beech-forest circuit through granite boulders, crags and the UNESCO-listed Jizera Mountains beech landscape, with an exceptional autumn transformation.

Why it is worth considering

Already A/D2 in ordinary season; at peak beech colour the combination of old forest, steep relief, granite and low autumn light can reach S-like experiential quality and D4 drive-worthiness. It is one of the few Czech seasonal transformations worth structuring a weekend around.

About

The loop from Oldřichov v Hájích climbs through the Jizerskohorské bučiny toward Skalní hrad and Oldřichovský Špičák. Unlike the broad plateau routes elsewhere in the Jizera Mountains, this side is steep, enclosed and rocky: roots, granite blocks, steps and short ladder-like passages sit inside mature beech forest. In the national nature reserve visitors must remain on designated paths.

Location: Oldřichov v Hájích · Liberecký kraj · Czechia
