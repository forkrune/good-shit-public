Nakaminato Fish Market

那珂湊おさかな市場

A working retail market with enough competitive seafood stalls and casual eating to reward careful browsing rather than a single famous order.

Why it is worth considering

The market is strongest as a flexible, inexpensive tasting route and a place to see what is actually abundant in Ibaraki waters.

About

Nakaminato Fish Market forms a compact row of fish retailers and restaurants near the port. It is visitor-friendly but still supports serious seafood purchasing and rapid product turnover.

Location: 那珂湊おさかな市場 — Hitachinaka, Ibaraki · Hitachinaka · Ibaraki · Japan
