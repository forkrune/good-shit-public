Kakizaki Shoten Kaisen Kobo

柿崎商店 海鮮工房

A fishmonger’s upstairs canteen where Hokkaidō seafood is served with unusually little distance between wholesale counter and bowl.

Why it is worth considering

The attraction is not refinement: it is access to strong raw material at a price and breadth difficult to reproduce in an ordinary city restaurant.

About

Kakizaki Shoten is both a busy seafood retailer and a plain second-floor dining room. The setting is functional, but the turnover, range and pricing make it one of the clearest producer-market-to-table stops in Yoichi.
