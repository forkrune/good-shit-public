Why it is worth considering

A high geological payoff for a short outing and roughly D1 travel from Prague. Milešovka is the larger panoramic landmark, while Bořeň delivers much more direct rock terrain per kilometre.

About

Bořeň is a freestanding volcanic hill above Bílina with an unusually rocky character for its modest elevation. The recommended loop uses the legal hiking approach to the summit and passes rough stone, steep natural steps and extensive rock walls rather than treating the hill as a roadside viewpoint. Dry conditions are strongly preferable; ice or wet rock changes the difficulty significantly.

Location: Bílina · Ústecký kraj · Czechia
