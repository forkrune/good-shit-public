Hirosaki Cidre Workshop kimori

弘前シードル工房 kimori

A small cidery inside Hirosaki Apple Park that turns the city’s orchard economy into a direct farm-to-fermentation visit.

Why it is worth considering

The physical link between orchard, local cultivars and fermentation makes this a more meaningful cider stop than a simple retail tasting.

About

kimori occupies a compact building amid working apple plots and explains both Hirosaki apple culture and its own cider production. Visitors can buy and taste producer-made cider in the landscape that supplies it rather than at an urban bottle shop.

Why it matters

Food / cultural background
Hirosaki is Japan’s dominant apple growing centre, and kimori represents a newer craft cider layer built directly onto that agricultural base.
