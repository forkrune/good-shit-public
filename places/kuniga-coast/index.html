Kuniga Coast

国賀海岸

Pastoral uplands fall directly into immense sea cliffs, arches and stacks, with the best experience coming from walking from Matengai down toward the lower coast.

Why it is worth considering

It is one of the strongest combinations found of sustained coastal walking, working pasture and vertical marine topography, and the ordinary trail delivers the same contrast seen in the famous imagery.

About

Kuniga Coast on Nishinoshima combines grazing cattle and horses with one of Japan's most dramatic cliff coasts. Its defining strength is continuity: open pasture at the roughly 257 m Matengai escarpment descends toward natural arches, rock stacks and the shore. It is not merely a drive-up viewpoint; the top-to-sea walk is the destination.
