Maruka Jozo & haccosido

丸加醸造場・haccosido（ハッコシド）

A working 1927 bean-miso and tamari brewery paired with a renovated miso-storehouse café, making it possible to see wooden vats and then taste the producer’s fermentation culture as a complete meal.

Why it is worth considering

Unlike a display-only factory or a fashionable café borrowing fermentation language, the production site, old vat material, shop and cooking are physically and operationally connected. A weekday visitor can pair a brief wooden-vat viewing with a meal that demonstrates how the seasonings work in ordinary food.

About

Maruka Jozo still produces bean miso, tamari and miso pickles beside Koshido Station. Its former miso storehouse has become haccosido, a small café serving carefully composed breakfasts, lunches and sweets built around fermented seasonings, while the adjoining shop provides direct access to the producer’s best-known mountain-burdock miso pickle and other products.

Reserve breakfast or one of the lunch sittings by telephone, then ask at the brewery shop about the free weekday vat viewing; finish by comparing the mountain burdock miso pickle with a less familiar seasonal pickle.

Why it matters

Food / cultural background
Founded in 1927, Maruka belongs to Mikawa’s bean miso and tamari culture. The café opened in a former miso storehouse in 2022 and reuses old vat boards in the interior, converting production heritage into an eating experience rather than a detached museum display.

Queue expectations
The small café can be fully reserved, especially at lunch; walk ins risk no table. Brewery viewing itself is brief and not queue driven.
Opening / seasonal considerations
As of April 2026, haccosido lists breakfast 08:00–10:00, lunch 11:00–15:00 and café 14:00–17:00, with Monday and Thursday closure. The brewery shop and viewing use a separate weekday schedule; reconfirm both calendars before travel.
Best timing / season
Reserve a weekday breakfast or lunch so the café and brewery viewing windows overlap; avoid arriving speculatively on a café closure day.
