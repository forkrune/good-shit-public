Klokočské skály–Kozákov–Rotštejn loop

Klokočské skály – Kozákov – Rotštejn

A 24 km Český ráj circuit that turns familiar sandstone geology into a serious hiking day by linking Klokočské průchody, Kozákov and Rotštejn.

Why it is worth considering

Český ráj is famous, but this route is a less conventional way to experience it and is much more substantial than the standard postcard loops. It earns D2 because the route itself remains rewarding across its connectors, not only at the famous nodes.

About

This official long route starts and ends at Turnov station and links Klokočské průchody, sandstone formations, Kozákov, Rotštejn and quieter connective terrain. Its value is the complete walking experience rather than any single attraction. It avoids the usual short headline-rock circuit and gives enough distance and ascent to feel like a full hiking day while remaining public-transport friendly.

Location: Turnov · Liberecký kraj · Czechia
