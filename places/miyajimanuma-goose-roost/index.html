Miyajimanuma Goose Roost Departure

宮島沼のマガンねぐら立ち

A small Hokkaido wetland that can become extraordinary when tens of thousands of greater white-fronted geese leave or return to the communal roost around dawn and dusk.

Bibai · Hokkaido · Japan

Editorial verdict

Why go

Major-detour worthwhile during the migration peak, usually mid-to-late April and late September to early October. Outside migration it should not consume scarce national travel time. Observe only from designated areas and follow local wildlife guidance.

Why it is worth the trip

What you actually experience

At migration peak, experience mass dawn departures or dusk returns of greater white fronted geese, with sound and moving formations transforming a small wetland.

Access & effort

Best conditions

Usually mid–late April and late September–early October, with current migration counts checked immediately before travel.

Current operational notes

Follow municipal wildlife guidance and current migration updates; do not approach roosting birds outside designated observation areas.

Challenge evidence

Limitations

Peak numbers and synchronized movement vary with weather and bird behaviour; fog/rain can fragment the spectacle.

Evidence against visiting

Outside migration the wetland is not destination worthy for a general traveller, and even in season a single perfectly synchronized departure is not guaranteed.
