Shokudo Kamecchi

食堂かめっち。

A community-run demonstration of tamago-kake gohan built around local eggs, rice and condiments rather than a contrived luxury version.

Why it is worth considering

It is extremely inexpensive and culturally revealing, though best treated as a regional stop rather than a major standalone detour.

About

Shokudo Kamecchi serves the elemental combination of hot rice and raw egg in Misaki, a town that promoted itself around egg production. The restaurant was renewed and reopened on August 3, 2026.

Location: 食堂かめっち。 — Misaki, Okayama · Misaki · Okayama · Japan
