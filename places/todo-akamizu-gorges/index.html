Todo & Akamizu Gorges

桃洞渓谷・赤水渓谷

Kilometres of smooth welded-tuff riverbed, potholes, shallow clear water and old beech forest turn the gorge floor itself into the walking experience.

Why it is worth considering

The unusual value is sustained contact with the stone-and-water floor of the gorge. In early to mid-October the polished rock, clear water and beech autumn colour create one of the research run's strongest destination-worthy landscapes.

About

In Oku-Moriyoshi, water has polished broad welded-tuff channels into a continuous walking environment rather than a sequence of isolated waterfalls. The maintained Todo route is the conservative version: roughly an 8 km return outing with little elevation. Akamizu is more immersive and repeatedly enters the stream; the full Todo–Akamizu connection can cross into technical canyon terrain and should not be inferred to be ordinary hiking.

Location: Oku-Moriyoshi, Kitaakita · Kitaakita · Akita · Japan
