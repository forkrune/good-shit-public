About

A quiet adult-oriented Okuhida stay pairing reliable privacy, snow and traditional mountain atmosphere. The qualifying private-bath arrangement is: Every room has a private outdoor bath and hinoki indoor bath; two detached riverside private baths provide the strongest openness. Water treatment is recorded as kakenagashi with heating. Couples privacy is assessed as highly private, with scenery/openness rated strong. Source water is heated/temperature-managed rather than untouched, and the best riverside baths have separate use rules.

Location: Fukuchi Onsen / Okuhida · Gifu · Japan
