About

A concentrated, austere fermented soybean preserves a temple-linked seasoning tradition very different from sticky breakfast natto. The key value lies in observing transformation over time—microbial, enzymatic, oxidative or preservative—and then tasting the result in context. The mapped entity is specifically Daitokuji temple precinct specialist; other branches are not counted separately.
