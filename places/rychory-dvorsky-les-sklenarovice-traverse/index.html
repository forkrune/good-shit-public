Rýchory–Dvorský les–Sklenářovice traverse

Rýchory – Dvorský les – Sklenářovické údolí

A quieter eastern-Krkonoše traverse through old beech woodland, mountain meadows, wetlands, mining traces and the vanished settlement landscape of Sklenářovice.

Why it is worth considering

Normally an A/D2 route, but peak beech colour combined with mist or low cloud can make the forest atmosphere worth a cross-country trip. It is much quieter and more enclosed than the iconic central-Krkonoše ridge experience.

About

The official point-to-point route runs from Svoboda nad Úpou across the Rýchory and Dvorský les area, then through the Sklenářovice landscape to Mladé Buky. It combines old woodland, meadow and wetland scenery with traces of mining and a vanished mountain settlement, so the historical layer improves an already strong landscape hike rather than serving as isolated archaeology.

Location: Svoboda nad Úpou · Královéhradecký kraj · Czechia
