About

A rare case where exceptional gorge scenery and source-fed private bathing remain available at regional rather than luxury-brand prices. The qualifying private-bath arrangement is: All nine rooms have gorge-facing outdoor baths; the detached suite adds two baths and the strongest separation. Water treatment is recorded as kakenagashi with heating. Couples privacy is assessed as highly private, with scenery/openness rated exceptional. The source is a cool sulfur/mineral spring and is heated; published reference prices need live confirmation.
