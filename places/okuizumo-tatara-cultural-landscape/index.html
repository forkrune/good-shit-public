Okuizumo Tatara Cultural Landscape

奥出雲たたら製鉄・棚田景観

A rural landscape where former iron-sand extraction terrain was deliberately reworked into rice terraces, channels and productive fields.

major-detourtatararice-terraceslandscape-reading

Why it is worth considering

Major-detour worthwhile for landscape/history interests, ideally as a half- or full-day car journey linking a tatara museum or furnace site with the agricultural terrain. Green rice season, harvest gold and autumn all work. The experience is weaker if treated as one viewpoint without context.

About

This is an unusually good example of industrial history becoming difficult to see precisely because landscape rehabilitation succeeded. Without interpretation it can look like merely attractive countryside; with a museum, guide or prior reading, residual hill forms, channels and terraced fields become evidence of the old kanna-nagashi iron-sand process.
