Why it is worth considering

Its strongest decision-relevant feature is access to Fresh Okinawan loose tofu eaten early near its production site. The recommended visit is: Arrive early and order the plain yushi-dofu set before adding strongly flavoured condiments.

About

Warm, barely set soy curds and their liquid are eaten as an everyday breakfast rather than a composed restaurant tofu dish. Everyday affordability and local routine are part of the value, but obscurity is not treated as quality evidence.
