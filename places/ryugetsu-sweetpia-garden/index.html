Ryugetsu Sweetpia Garden

柳月スイートピア・ガーデン

A well-designed confectionery factory stop where production viewing, fresh cake and Tokachi ingredients form a coherent experience.

Why it is worth considering

The stop offers unusually good value for families or confectionery enthusiasts and pairs naturally with other Tokachi dairy destinations.

About

Sweetpia Garden is Ryugetsu’s visitor-facing factory, shop and café near Obihiro. It is more meaningful than an ordinary brand outlet because the production line and fresh product range explain how a mass-market Hokkaidō sweet is made.

Location: 柳月スイートピア・ガーデン — Otofuke, Hokkaido · Otofuke · Hokkaido · Japan
