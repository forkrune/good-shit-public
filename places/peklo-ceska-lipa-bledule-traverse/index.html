Peklo u České Lípy

A sandstone stream-gorge traverse that changes from a good local walk into a much stronger destination when spring snowflakes carpet the wet valley floor.

Why it is worth considering

Normally B/D1, but a well-timed spring-snowflake peak can reach A/D2. It is more visually complete than many flower-only destinations because the botanical event sits inside an already atmospheric sandstone-and-water landscape.

About

The line from Zahrádky toward Dubice passes Karba and the protected Peklo valley along Robečský potok. Sandstone walls, wet woodland and boardwalk-like passages give the gorge year-round structure; the major seasonal uplift comes when large populations of spring snowflake flower across the valley bottom. Visitors must stay on the route and avoid stepping into flower stands, especially during the crowded peak.

Location: Zahrádky / Česká Lípa · Liberecký kraj · Czechia
