Padrť ponds and vanished-villages loop

Padrťské rybníky a zaniklé obce

A long but easy Brdy circuit through a broad mountain basin, two major ponds and the legible empty landscape of villages erased by the former military area.

Why it is worth considering

A/D2 rather than merely a pleasant Brdy outing: the landscape is spacious and quiet, while the historical-map layer remains readable for hours instead of collapsing into one memorial plaque. It is difficult to substitute with a closer generic forest walk.

About

The circuit from the Kolvín side combines the Padrť pond basin with the landscapes of the vanished settlements Padrť, Přední Záběhlá, Zadní Záběhlá and Kolvín. Its power comes from the interaction of water, wet meadows, forest roads and historical absence: old settlement names and route alignments still make sense on the ground even though normal village life disappeared. The route stays on mapped public roads and paths; exploring off-road military remnants is neither needed nor recommended.

Location: Kolvín / Skořice · Plzeňský kraj · Czechia
