Koshikishima Cliff Coast

甑島列島

An island-scale wall of stratified, tilted and folded sea cliffs combines with barrier lakes, villages and road viewpoints; a small-boat cruise adds the strongest sense of scale.

Why it is worth considering

This was one of the research run's credible E5 landscapes: a coherent island-scale geological coast whose science transfers directly into spectacle, while still retaining worthwhile terrestrial scenery when boats cannot sail.

About

The Koshiki Islands are valuable as a geological system rather than one formation. Long western cliff walls expose sedimentary layers at monumental scale while the opposite side adds lagoons, sand bars, settlements and bridge engineering. Land travel is substantial enough to justify the trip on its own, but calm-sea cliff cruises reveal structures and vertical scale that are hard to read from shore.

Location: Koshiki Islands · Satsumasendai · Kagoshima · Japan
