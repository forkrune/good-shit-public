Why it is worth considering

Its strongest decision-relevant feature is access to Obuse chestnut culture and the ultra-seasonal fresh chestnut confection Suzaku. The recommended visit is: For a deliberate trip, secure the seasonal Suzaku service; outside season, compare multiple chestnut preparations in the main shop.

About

Suzaku strips away sugar-heavy pastry structure and presents freshly harvested chestnut as a fragile seasonal food tied tightly to Obuse. Freshness, starch/sugar technique, season and local ritual matter more than souvenir prestige. The mapped entity is specifically Obuse main shop and seasonal Suzaku service; other branches are not counted separately.
