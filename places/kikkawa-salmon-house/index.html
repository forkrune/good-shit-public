Why it is worth considering

Its strongest decision-relevant feature is access to Murakami's whole-salmon preservation and ritual use. The recommended visit is: Visit the shop first for interpretation, then taste a meal at Izutsuya to understand the products in use.

About

The hanging-salmon interior and exceptionally broad use of the fish reveal Murakami as a preservation system, not merely a place that serves salmon. The production process must be visible or interpretable enough to justify a visitor destination rather than a factory address.
