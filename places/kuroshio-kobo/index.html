About

Visitors can see or perform the violent, aromatic straw sear that defines high-quality Kochi tataki rather than receiving an anonymous sliced plate. Species, landing system, season and handling are more important than generic claims of freshness. The mapped entity is specifically Nakatosa coastal bonito workshop/restaurant; other branches are not counted separately.
