Nameratoko Gorge

滑床渓谷

A long granite gorge of white slabs, clear pools, channels and broad sliding waterfalls in southwest Shikoku.

Matsuno · Ehime · Japan

Editorial take

Why go

Strong regional discovery when approached as a gorge landscape rather than a single waterfall. Dry-to-normal water, warm-season greenery and enough time to walk beyond one viewpoint produce the best reward.

What you actually experience

Follow a long pale granite gorge of clear pools, channels, slabs and broad sliding falls rather than treating one named waterfall as the whole destination.

Access & effort

Map geometry note
Point represents the Nameratoko gorge zone, not a canyoning route.

Effort required

Several hours on the normal gorge outing; guided canyoning is a separate optional activity requiring legitimate operators and suitable conditions.

Best conditions

Dry to normal water in the green season or autumn; enough time to walk beyond a single viewpoint.

Current operational notes

Evaluate the broader gorge, not only Yukiwa Falls; use legitimate operators for any canyoning.

Challenge evidence

Limitations

Water level changes the look substantially, and adventure variants require appropriate guides and equipment.

Evidence against visiting

Waterfall specialists report that Yukiwa Falls can lack power in low water. The named waterfall alone does not justify the journey.
