Nameratoko Gorge

滑床渓谷

A long granite gorge of white slabs, clear pools, channels and broad sliding waterfalls in southwest Shikoku.

strong-regionalgraniteclear-poolswaterfalls

Why it is worth considering

Strong regional discovery when approached as a gorge landscape rather than a single waterfall. Dry-to-normal water, warm-season greenery and enough time to walk beyond one viewpoint produce the best reward.

About

Validation downgraded the famous Yukiwa Falls as a standalone target: specialist waterfall evidence reports that it can lack power in low water. The broader gorge survives that criticism because the granite river environment continues for kilometres and can be experienced as a walk or, with a legitimate operator and suitable conditions, canyoning.

Location: Nameratoko Gorge / Ashizuri-Uwakai National Park · Matsuno · Ehime · Japan
