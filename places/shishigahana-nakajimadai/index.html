Shishigahana Wetland & Nakajimadai Forest

獅子ヶ鼻湿原・中島台

An intimate Chōkai foothill ecosystem of giant pollarded beeches, abundant cold springs and a globally unusual moss community.

strong-regionalgiant-beechspringsbotany

Why it is worth considering

A strong regional discovery rather than a national must-see for everyone. It becomes much more compelling for botany, forest ecology or vernacular woodland-use interests. Stay on boardwalks and marked routes; confirm any damaged or restricted sections before visiting.

About

The immediate visual payoff comes from the huge multi-stemmed beeches and spring-fed forest. The rare “Chōkai marimo” moss community is scientifically remarkable but visually subtler and benefits strongly from interpretation. The boardwalk/forest circuit is roughly 5 km and 2–3 hours.

Location: Nakajimadai Recreation Forest · Nikaho · Akita · Japan

Limitations
The rare moss is visually subtle; boardwalk damage or ecological restrictions can affect access.
Evidence against visiting
Scientific rarity does not translate automatically into spectacle. Without context, visitors may simply see a pleasant wet forest and unusual trees.
