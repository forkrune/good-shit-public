About

A roughly 400-year-old registered historic inn places a private bath directly on the river at an unusually low total cost. The qualifying private-bath arrangement is: The destination-worthy private experience is the reservable outdoor bath immediately beside the Hidaka River; rooms are not uniformly outdoor-bath equipped. Water treatment is recorded as natural onsen treatment unclear. Couples privacy is assessed as highly private, with scenery/openness rated exceptional. Confirm reservation rules, duration, fee and guaranteed access; water treatment remains unclear and booking is old-fashioned.
