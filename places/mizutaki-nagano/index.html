Mizutaki Nagano

水たき 長野

A disciplined, broth-first version of Hakata mizutaki where staff build the meal from concentrated chicken stock through meat, vegetables and final rice.

Why it is worth considering

The meal is affordable relative to its depth and offers a regional hotpot experience more focused than many tourist-oriented alternatives.

About

Mizutaki Nagano is a specialist rather than a broad Hakata restaurant. The progression makes clear how gelatinous chicken broth, ponzu and vegetables are intended to work together.

Location: 水たき 長野 — Fukuoka, Fukuoka · Fukuoka · Fukuoka · Japan
