Boreč ventarole winter loop

Boreč

A tiny volcanic hill whose fissure-ventilation phenomenon turns an otherwise modest walk into a disproportionately interesting winter geological excursion during hard frost.

Why it is worth considering

Normally only a B/D0.5–D1 outing, but during a properly cold spell the unusual ventarole phenomenon can justify D2 travel from Prague. It is a compact geological experience rather than a summit-view hike.

About

The Boreč nature trail is short and easy in ordinary conditions, but the destination changes character in sustained winter cold. Warm, humid air moving through the fractured volcanic hill can emerge from summit fissures and become conspicuous against freezing ambient air. The phenomenon is condition-driven rather than date-driven; snow and ice can also make the little trail substantially slipperier.

Location: Režný Újezd · Ústecký kraj · Czechia
