Boreč–Holý vrch–Košťálov winter loop

Boreč – Holý vrch – Košťálov

A fuller České středohoří circuit that combines Boreč’s frost-driven ventarole phenomenon with open steppe terrain on Holý vrch and the castle hill of Košťálov.

Why it is worth considering

Normally B/D1 as a varied volcanic-landscape loop; during several genuinely cold days Boreč’s ventaroles can raise the outing to A-like quality and D2 drive-worthiness. The longer route fixes the main weakness of the old three-kilometre recommendation: too little hiking substance.

About

The expanded loop starts at Režný Újezd, climbs Boreč on the established nature trail, then links Holý vrch and Košťálov using the mapped path network. Boreč remains the condition-driven highlight: during sustained frost, relatively warm humid air can emerge from fissures and produce conspicuous vapour, frost and locally snow-free vegetation. The longer circuit turns the phenomenon from a short geological stop into a satisfying half-day hike. Ice can materially increase difficulty.

Location: Režný Újezd · Ústecký kraj · Czechia
