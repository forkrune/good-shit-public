Hradčanské stěny–Psí kostel–Skalní brána loop

Hradčanské stěny – Psí kostel – Skalní brána

A quiet sandstone circuit through pine forest, cliff edges, an unusually large rock overhang and a natural rock gate in the former Ralsko military landscape.

Why it is worth considering

A/D2 for roughly D1 travel friction. It gives much of the sandstone-world feeling associated with famous Czech rock cities while being quieter, stranger and more exploratory, without needing technical climbing or entry into unsafe military ruins.

About

From Hradčany the route links the Hradčanské stěny viewpoints with Psí kostel and Skalní brána. The appeal is less one monumental tower than the sustained atmosphere: sandy paths, undercut sandstone walls, sparse pine woods and large natural cavities with very little urban interruption. A short unmarked connection should only be used while it remains a mapped public path; if forestry or protection closures affect it, return on the marked route rather than improvising cross-country.
