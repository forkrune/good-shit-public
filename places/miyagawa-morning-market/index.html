Miyagawa Morning Market

宮川朝市

A compact riverside morning market that rewards sampling local pickles, miso, fruit and prepared foods before tourist crowds dominate the old town.

Why it is worth considering

Its destination value is moderate but real because it exposes the food supply of the Hida basin in an easy central setting.

About

Miyagawa Morning Market mixes long-standing local vendors with increasingly tourist-oriented stalls. It remains worthwhile when approached as a source of agricultural products and conversation with producers rather than street-food grazing alone.

Location: 宮川朝市 — Takayama, Gifu · Takayama · Gifu · Japan
