Oslava: Náměšť–Lamberk–Kraví Hora traverse

Údolí Oslavy – Lamberk – Kraví Hora

A long railway-to-railway traverse through the wild Oslava valley, linking riverside paths, two atmospheric castle ruins, old mill landscapes and repeated steep valley-side climbs.

Why it is worth considering

A genuine A/D2 long-day hike. It is farther from Prague than the highest-return anomalies, but the length and continuity make it difficult to substitute with a shorter river outing. The point-to-point rail geometry is a feature, not a compromise.

About

The preferred route starts at Náměšť nad Oslavou station, follows the valley through Lamberk and the protected Oslava landscape, continues toward Kraví Hora and exits to Rapotice station. Its strength is continuity: for many hours the river, steep wooded slopes, ruins and former mill sites form one journey rather than a chain of isolated POIs. Elevated water can make riverside sections harder or unsafe, so normal or low-to-moderate flow is preferable to dramatic flood conditions.

Location: Náměšť nad Oslavou / Rapotice · Kraj Vysočina · Czechia
