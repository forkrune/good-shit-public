Čertoryje orchid meadow loop

Čertoryje – Orchidejová naučná stezka

A White Carpathian meadow loop that becomes a national-level destination during the late-spring orchid and flower peak, when species-rich grassland and solitary trees align.

Why it is worth considering

This is the clearest seasonal transformation in the Czech research. Ordinary quality is around A/D2; a strong late-May to mid-June flowering peak can justify D4 and a dedicated cross-country trip.

About

The route from Lučina follows the Orchidejová nature-trail circuit through the Čertoryje landscape and related meadow sites. The reserve is exceptionally species-rich, but its catalogue value is strongly seasonal: outside the flowering period it is a very good meadow landscape; during the right late-spring window the visual and ecological experience can jump to S-tier quality. Visitors must stay on designated paths in the reserve and should not approach individual flowers off trail.

Location: Tvarožná Lhota · Jihomoravský kraj · Czechia
