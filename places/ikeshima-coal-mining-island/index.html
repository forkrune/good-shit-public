Ikeshima Coal-Mining Island

池島炭鉱

A still-inhabited former company town pairs a guided underground mine trolley experience with unusually direct surface access to Japan's postwar coal-mining landscape.

Why it is worth considering

Hashima dominates the mining-island narrative, but Ikeshima offers more direct engagement with lived mining history. The underground experience is scheduled to end at the close of March 2027, making current planning time-critical.

About

Ikeshima is not simply another abandoned mining island. Visitors can enter preserved underground workings on a fixed guided experience, historically led by former miners, then walk through a contracting but still inhabited company settlement containing housing and industrial remains. This combination of machinery, oral testimony, underground space and everyday island fabric made it the research run's most urgent industrial discovery.

Location: Ikeshima, offshore from Sotome · Nagasaki City · Nagasaki · Japan
