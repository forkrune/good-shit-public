Ralsko and Vranovské skály loop

Ralsko a Vranovské skály

A wild-feeling loop from Mimoň that shifts from sandstone formations to basalt summit terrain and castle ruins inside the former Ralsko military landscape.

Why it is worth considering

The combination of geology, ruins, forest and former military landscape feels much stranger and wilder than its roughly D1 travel time from Prague suggests. Bezděz has the more intact castle; this route has the more unusual landscape journey.

About

The route starts at Mimoň railway station, passes Vranov and the publicly accessible Juliina vyhlídka, climbs through increasingly rugged terrain to the Ralsko summit ruins, then returns through forest. The appeal is the transition between sandstone, basalt boulder and wall terrain, woodland and the former-military cultural landscape. The route does not require scrambling onto protected sandstone towers.

Location: Mimoň · Liberecký kraj · Czechia
