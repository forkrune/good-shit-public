Why it is worth considering

It offers both a high-quality meal and legible craft process, making the detour much stronger than buying packaged Inaniwa udon.

About

Sato Yosuke’s head store in Inaniwa combines a restaurant, shop and production context in the noodle’s home region. The thin, hand-stretched noodles show exceptional smoothness and resilience when served simply.

Location: 佐藤養助 総本店 — Yuzawa, Akita · Yuzawa · Akita · Japan
