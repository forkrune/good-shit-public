Why it is worth considering

Thin rice sheets are steamed to order and paired with pork, wood-ear mushroom, herbs, chả and dipping sauce; phở cuốn provides a second rice-sheet preparation. Order: Bánh cuốn; add phở cuốn if sharing. Why: Fresh steaming and texture are the point and are best judged immediately at the stall.
