Uryūnuma Wetland Circuit

雨竜沼湿原

A real mountain approach opens abruptly into a broad high moor of pools, winding channels and boardwalk, making the wetland feel far larger than a roadside marsh.

Why it is worth considering

The transition from enclosed forest climb to a wide, elevated moor is the experience. Flower season and September autumn grass both give the pools and mountain enclosure enough scale to justify a dedicated detour.

About

The route climbs from Uryūnuma Gate Park before entering the high wetland and looping the boardwalk among ponds and the meandering Penkepetan River. The bundled line geometry is the current wetland circuit only. It intentionally does not continue over the South Shokanbetsu–Shokanbetsu segment, which was closed for the 2026 season.

Location: Uryūnuma Gate Park and Uryūnuma Wetland · Uryū · Hokkaido · Japan
