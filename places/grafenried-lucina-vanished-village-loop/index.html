Grafenried/Lučina vanished-village loop

Grafenried / Lučina

A Český les borderland walk where a substantial vanished village remains physically legible through exposed foundations, cemetery traces and the surrounding depopulated landscape.

Why it is worth considering

The historical-map anomaly survives the real visitor-quality test: the absence of the old settlement is physically legible rather than merely archival. It earns A quality for historical landscape, though the actual Prague drive is slightly greater than the D2 experience justifies as a standalone trip.

About

This loop from Mýtnice approaches the former village of Grafenried/Lučina through the Czech-German border landscape. Unlike many historical-map curiosities, enough building foundations, cemetery and settlement structure survive or have been exposed that the erased village can be understood on the ground. The hike is modest by itself; its strongest use is as part of a broader Český les historical-landscape weekend.

Location: Nemanice · Plzeňský kraj · Czechia
