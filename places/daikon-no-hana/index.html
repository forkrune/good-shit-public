About

A low-density woodland retreat with excellent food, credible source water and multiple private-bathing settings rather than one token balcony tub. The qualifying private-bath arrangement is: Only explicitly outdoor room categories such as Buna, Momiji and Nazuna-style rooms. Ito-tonbo and Tsumekusa are windowed indoor baths; several rooms have no private bath. Water treatment is recorded as kakenagashi water added for temperature. Couples privacy is assessed as highly private, with scenery/openness rated exceptional. Property-wide marketing is broader than actual eligibility; book only an explicitly outdoor room. Some source water may be adjusted with water for temperature.

Location: Togatta Onsen / Zao · Miyagi · Japan
