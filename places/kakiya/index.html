About

Kakiya is a focused oyster restaurant on Miyajima’s main street. It is polished and visitor-friendly, but the breadth of technique and sourcing make it stronger than the island’s many quick oyster stands.

Location: 牡蠣屋 — Hatsukaichi, Hiroshima · Hatsukaichi · Hiroshima · Japan
