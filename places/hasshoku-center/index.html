Hasshoku Center

八食センター

A serious regional food market where visitors can buy seafood from multiple vendors and grill it themselves in the Shichirin Village.

Why it is worth considering

The market offers control over ingredient choice and a direct link between regional supply and meal, making it more than a shopping complex.

About

Hasshoku Center is a large market serving both residents and visitors. Its defining experience is purchasing seafood directly from stalls and cooking it over charcoal in a communal grill area.

Location: 八食センター — Hachinohe, Aomori · Hachinohe · Aomori · Japan
