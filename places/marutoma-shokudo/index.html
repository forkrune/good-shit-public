Why it is worth considering

The combination of regional ingredient, working-port context and very low meal price justifies the queue.

About

Marutoma Shokudo sits by Tomakomai’s fishing port and is famous for surf-clam dishes rather than generic seafood. The menu is unusually broad and playful, but the underlying reason to visit is the city’s exceptional hokki supply.
