Separate lifecycle

Publication is not inferred from tier alone

A historically excellent entity can close, deteriorate or require reverification without losing its immutable ID.

candidate→needs review→published→deprecated / closed

Only editorial.status = published and tier S/A/B/C enter generated search, browse, map, tier lists or detail pages. A publish status with D/E/F/Unranked is a validation error.

“No curated places here yet”

That message never claims an area contains no restaurants, trails, venues or attractions. It says only that no current published record is known to clear this catalogue’s bar.

Good Shit — absence is normal; publication means there is a positive reason to go.
